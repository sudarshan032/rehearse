## 3. One track, one project

- FAQ: "Can I enter more than one track? No. Pick one track, Builders or No-Code, and submit one project in it."
- **Builders:** "A working software product that solves a problem in your own work. Dictate requirements and follow-ups into your coding tools, and let them do the coding." For: full-stack developers, AI engineers, mobile developers, DevOps engineers.
- **No-Code:** "A working tool for your own job, made with an AI app builder or assistant, no coding needed. The judges have to be able to open and use it: an app, a shared assistant or a workflow, not a chat thread."
- Both: "Either way you ship a working tool, built by voice, and one real result it produced."
- The blog still uses the old name "At Work" for No-Code. Use the main page names.

## 4. Eligibility and cost

- FAQ: "Anyone in India. Professionals across all domains are welcome … Students are welcome as well."
- Terms §2: you must be 18 or older (or have parent/guardian consent). Terms §3: "Each person may hold one account."
- FAQ: "A Mac or Windows computer with a microphone, and a Wispr Flow account."
- FAQ: free to enter; "1 month of Wispr Flow Pro free". Other tools need your own accounts: "Their free plans are enough … You don't have to pay for anything to take part."
- Blog: sign up for Wispr Flow "with the same email you register for No Hands with." "Run your first Flow … Once that is verified, you are on the public leaderboard and in the competition."
- Main page step 1: "make your first Flow the start of your project: say the problem in your work you want to fix and what your tool should do."
- FAQ: "Why do I set up Wispr before I register? … Your prompts, specs and docs are spoken, not typed, so Flow has to be working before you start building."

## 5. "Your own work" and new work

- Main page: "fix a real problem in their own work by building a tool for it."
- Ideas page: "These are ideas to get you started, not a list to pick from. The best entry fixes a problem from your own work."
- Judging: "A specific problem, a clear user, and a tool that person would keep using."
- FAQ: any editor, AI assistant or app builder is allowed; "Wispr Flow is how you talk to them."
- FAQ: "Use your voice for as much as you can: prompts, docs, messages and commit notes. You can still use the keyboard for shortcuts and small fixes. **Judges look at how much of your writing went through Flow.**"
- **Terms §5 (applies to every WeMakeDevs hackathon):** "No cheating. This includes submitting someone else's work as your own, plagiarizing existing projects, or **misrepresenting what you built during the hackathon window**." Breaking it can mean disqualification "at any time, including after judging or after a winner has been announced, and without prior notice". Decisions "are final".
- So: **build everything new, inside the window, in a fresh repo**, and say honestly what is new. No old code, no client code.

## 6. Ownership

- FAQ: "You do. WeMakeDevs and Wispr have the right to feature your project and demo video on their channels."
- Terms §6: you keep full ownership; WeMakeDevs and the hackathon's sponsors get a "limited, non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to access, display, demonstrate, and evaluate your Submission for purposes of judging, event promotion, and showcasing".
- So nothing confidential (client names, images, data) may appear in the project, demo or video.

## 9. Submitting: one shot, no editing

- What to submit (main page): "A link to your working project." / "A demo video of up to two minutes." / "Your Wispr usage card, as a screenshot from Insights in the desktop app." / "A few lines on the problem, who it is for and how you used Wispr."
- Blog also: "a short clip of Flow in use". We put it inside the video.
- Judges must be able to try it "without paying or asking you for access: share a public link, and if it needs a sign-in, put a test login and sample input in your description."
- **Editing after submitting: not allowed.** No page mentions editing, and Siddhu checked the signed-in site on 23 Oct and found no edit option. **Treat the submission as final. Submit once, when everything is finished.**
- Because the live link and repo keep running after submission, nothing may break or change in a way that contradicts what we submitted.

## 10. Fairness and disputes

- Scores by multiple judges, averaged, all public with written comments; judges skip projects they are connected to.
- Scores published Wed 4 Nov; questions until the end of Thu 5 Nov, with a written reply.
- Winners' Wispr accounts verified with Wispr.
- Code of Conduct applies to all events, repos and channels: no plagiarism, no spam, respect others' IP.
