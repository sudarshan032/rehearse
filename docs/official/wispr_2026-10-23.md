07 / FAQ
## Got questions?
What is No Hands?
A two-week online hackathon from WeMakeDevs and Wispr Flow, from 16 to 30 October. Build a tool that solves a problem in your own work, and use Wispr Flow to speak your prompts, instructions and docs as you build.
Get 1 month of Wispr Flow Pro free <https://wisprflow.ai/?promo_code=WEMAKEDEVS&utm_source=wemakedevs&utm_medium=partner&utm_campaign=wemakedevs&dub_id=1oU35MQWSE7nktLN>
Who can take part?
Anyone in India. Professionals across all domains are welcome: founders, developers, marketing, product, HR and others. Students are welcome as well. It's a solo challenge, so no teams: you register, set up Wispr and build on your own.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. The No-Code track is built for people who don't write code, whatever your role: marketing, sales, HR, ops, design or anything else. You build your tool with an AI app builder like Lovable or an assistant like ChatGPT or Claude, and tell it what to do by talking through Wispr Flow.
What counts as a No-Code entry?
A tool someone else can open and use. An app made with an AI app builder like Lovable counts, and so does a reusable assistant, such as a custom GPT or a Gemini Gem shared by link, or a workflow in Zapier, Make or Notion that runs when the judges trigger it, from a form for example. A chat thread or a one-off prompt doesn't count. Judges need to try it without paying or asking you for access: share a public link, and if it needs a sign-in, put a test login and sample input in your description.
Does it cost anything?
No. The challenge is free to enter, and your Wispr Flow link includes 1 month of Wispr Flow Pro free.
What do I need?
A Mac or Windows computer with a microphone, and a Wispr Flow account.
Why do I set up Wispr before I register?
Because the challenge is about solving problems in a voice-first way. Your prompts, specs and docs are spoken, not typed, so Flow has to be working before you start building.
Do I have to use only my voice?
Use your voice for as much as you can: prompts, docs, messages and commit notes. You can still use the keyboard for shortcuts and small fixes. Judges look at how much of your writing went through Flow.
Can I use other tools?
Yes. Use any editor, AI assistant or app builder you like. Wispr Flow is how you talk to them.
Does the free month cover the other tools?
No. The free month is Wispr Flow Pro only. Lovable, Cursor, ChatGPT, Claude and the rest need your own accounts. Their free plans are enough for the examples on this page, but they cap how many messages or credits you get a day, so start early. You don't have to pay for anything to take part.
Can I enter more than one track?
No. Pick one track, Builders or No-Code, and submit one project in it.
How is Wispr usage measured?
From the usage card in your Wispr Flow desktop app, under Insights and Your usage. Winners' numbers are confirmed with Wispr before prizes are sent.
What if I disagree with my score?
Every score and comment is public. You can raise a question until the end of Thursday 5 November and you will get a written reply before winners are confirmed.
When are prizes sent?
Prize money is sent on Friday 6 November, the day winners are announced.
Who owns what I build?
You do. WeMakeDevs and Wispr have the right to feature your project and demo video on their channels.
Can I join after 16 October?
Yes. Registration stays open until submissions close on Friday 30 October.
