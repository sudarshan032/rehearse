## Two weeks to build, one to judge
Building starts on Friday 16 October and submissions close on Friday 30 October at 11:59 pm IST. The final scores and winners land the week after.
Fri 16 Oct
Challenge announced. Registration opens and building starts.
Fri 30 Oct, 11:59 pm IST
Submissions close.
Wed 4 Nov
Judges' scores and comments published on the leaderboard.
Thu 5 Nov
Last day to raise a question about a score.
Fri 6 Nov
Winners announced and prize money sent.
Registration stays open until submissions close, so you can join late.
