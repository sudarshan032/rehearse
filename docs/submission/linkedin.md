At work I build phone voice bots in English, Hindi and Telugu. One of my sales bots went live in one day, then took about 3 weeks of real calls before it stopped breaking its rules. Every mistake was found by a caller.

So for #NoHandsChallenge I built Rehearse: you build, rehearse and fix a voice bot just by speaking.

The workflow:
1. I hold the Wispr key and describe the bot, like briefing a teammate.
2. It asks a few questions, then draws the call flow, the rules and the prompt.
3. I play the caller. When the bot breaks a rule, the line turns red.
4. I say the fix, and the same caller line is replayed.
5. Then a real voice call in the browser, in Hindi and Telugu too.

On my own bot, the first rule break showed up 3.1 minutes after the brief, before any caller. Before, that took about 3 weeks.

I built it the same way: every prompt to Claude Code was spoken with Wispr Flow. [CARD WORDS] words dictated.
