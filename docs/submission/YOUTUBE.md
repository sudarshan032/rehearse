My voice bot broke a rule in 3.1 minutes of rehearsal. Before, finding a mistake like that took me about 3 weeks of real and test calls.
Rehearse lets you build, rehearse and fix a phone voice bot just by speaking, in Telugu, Hindi and English.

Try it: https://rehearse-ecru.vercel.app
No sign-in. A sample bot is ready on the first screen. You can also type.

What Rehearse does
- Describe the bot out loud, like briefing a teammate.
- It asks what it needs to know. You answer out loud.
- It draws the call flow, the rules and the prompt.
- Rehearse: play the caller. The path lights up, and a broken rule turns red on the exact line.
- Say the fix. Replay the same line and check the rule now holds.
- Run a real voice call in the browser, in English, Hindi and Telugu.
- Export the bot for the voice platform you use.

How I used Wispr Flow
- I hold the fn key and speak. Wispr Flow types my words into Rehearse. No typing in the whole demo.
- The brief, the answers and the fixes in this video are all spoken.
- I also built Rehearse by speaking to my coding tool with Wispr Flow: 52,628 words in 595 dictations from 23 to 27 Oct (Wispr Flow history on my Mac).
