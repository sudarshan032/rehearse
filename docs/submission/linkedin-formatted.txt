At work I build phone voice bots in English, Hindi and Telugu. One of my sales bots went live in one day, then took about 3 weeks of real calls before it stopped breaking its rules. Every mistake was found by a caller.

So for #NoHandsChallenge I built 𝗥𝗲𝗵𝗲𝗮𝗿𝘀𝗲: you build, rehearse and fix a voice bot just by speaking.

𝗛𝗼𝘄 𝗶𝘁 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸𝘀
🎙️ I hold the Wispr key and describe the bot, like briefing a teammate.
🧭 It asks a few questions, then draws the call flow, the rules and the prompt.
🎭 I play the caller. When the bot breaks a rule, the line turns red.
🔁 I say the fix, and the same caller line is replayed.
📞 Then a real voice call in the browser, in Hindi and Telugu too.
